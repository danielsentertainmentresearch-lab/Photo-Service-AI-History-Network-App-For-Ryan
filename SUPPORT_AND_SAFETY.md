# How support works in EventLens

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

EventLens asks you to write about how your life felt, not only what
happened. That means love, grief, loss and the hardest days of your life
can end up in it. So EventLens is built on harm reduction: no judgement,
nothing to earn or qualify for, and you stay in control of your own
support. This page explains every support feature, how it works and why it
exists.

## What EventLens is, and what it is not

EventLens is a place to record and understand your life. It is **not** a
crisis service, therapy, medical care or a monitoring service. No one
watches what you write. It doesn't diagnose anything, and it can't know
everything you're going through.

**If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency
number (911 in the US) or 988.**

## The Support page

The Support page is always one tap from the home screen, whatever is
going on and whether or not anything in the app suggested it. It shows:

- the crisis line and emergency number for your country, first and big;
- help if someone else is a danger to you;
- more crisis lines, mental health support (including NAMI and SAMHSA),
  and safer-use resources (including Never Use Alone and naloxone);
- a short grounding exercise you can do right there.

Nothing about your use of the Support page is recorded, apart from a
count of how often it is opened, with nothing about who opened it or why.
The services listed are independent organisations. EventLens doesn't run
them and isn't responsible for them.

## The quiet support line

When you finish a free write, the AI already reads it to make your
labels. It also notices whether you might need support. It reads for
meaning, the way a person would: what your words add up to, including
what is implied rather than said. It doesn't look for a list of words,
because any phrase can carry a weight only you know.

If it notices signs that you might be struggling, one quiet line appears
on that event: "If anything here is weighing on you, support is one tap
away." You can open Support or put the line away with "Not now".

What does **not** happen:

- No pop-up, no alarm, nothing blocked.
- No one is told. Nothing is sent anywhere because of it, and nothing is
  counted.
- It is never shown as a diagnosis or a judgement.

The AI can be wrong, both ways. It may miss what you meant, or read
metaphor, lyrics or a story as being about you. That is why the line is
gentle and easy to dismiss, and why the Support page is always there
anyway. What the AI noticed is kept only on your phone, with the event,
and is never exported.

## Reaching Dan, a peer supporter (coming in a later update)

Dan, who created EventLens, is a peer supporter trained in trauma-informed
care, crisis intervention and harm reduction. In a later update you will
be able to choose, only if you want to, to let him reach out to you
personally:

- It is off unless you turn it on.
- Nothing is ever sent automatically. If what you write ever says plainly
  that you are thinking of ending your life or of severely harming
  yourself, the app gently asks whether you would like Dan to reach out.
  Only if you say yes is anything sent.
- You choose how (call, text or email) and when.
- What you wrote is shared with him, so he doesn't go in blind, the same
  way crisis lines learn what is going on before someone joins the call.

Dan is one person, not an emergency service, and not available at every
hour. The crisis lines are always there first.

## As usual, Quiet and Honored

Tap a person's or place's name to choose how they are kept in your story.
The three choices sit side by side, and you can change them any time, in
any direction:

- **As usual**: nothing special.
- **Quiet**: they don't come up on their own: not on "What the AI is
  learning", not in sneak-peek insights, not in "On this day". Nothing is
  deleted. Your events, photos and writing stay exactly as they are.
- **Honored**: they are kept close. They have a place under "Remembering",
  your moments with them are gently marked in "On this day", and the AI
  writes about them with continuity and care.

Why: grief can be ambushed by a name. Quiet gives relief from that without
erasing anyone. And as long as someone isn't forgotten, they stay alive in
every memory they are part of. Honoring someone can help a wound of grief
become a scar. The app never suggests either one. The choice is yours, in
your own time.

## Erasing what the AI remembers

You can erase everything the AI remembers, all at once, from the privacy
policy (Settings, then Privacy policy). It can't be undone, and it doesn't
remove anyone from your events.

## Limits

EventLens offers information and ways to reach independent services. It
does not provide medical, psychological or emergency care, and it can't
guarantee that it will notice when you need support. Please use the
crisis lines or your emergency number whenever you need them.
